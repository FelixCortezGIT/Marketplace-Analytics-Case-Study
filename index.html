Marketplace Analytics Case Study

Multi-country ecommerce analytics project focused on marketplace performance, profitability monitoring, and operational insights.

Analyzing marketplace sales data across Amazon, Allegro, and Kaufland using Python and data visualization techniques.

Project Overview

This project simulates an internal ecommerce reporting workflow used to evaluate marketplace performance across multiple countries and sales channels.

The analysis combines transactional data, product information, and country-level metrics to generate business insights focused on revenue, profitability, operational risks, and sales trends.
